# Business Rules

| ID | Business Rule | Affected Tables and Operations | Enforcement Mechanism | Testing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SC01 | When an account is registered by email, its full name, email address, and password are required. Password confirmation must match the password, and the user must agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Only email-registered accounts must have a hashed password; accounts registered through Google, Facebook, or LinkedIn may have no local password. Email addresses must be unique. | `user_account` — INSERT, UPDATE | `UNIQUE` and `CHECK` constraints; application-level validation for password confirmation and agreement to the terms. | Test duplicate email addresses; email registration without a password; OAuth registration without a local password; missing password confirmation or agreement. |
| SC02 | A candidate must select at least one and at most five job categories as professional roles of interest. Every selected category must be a Specialisation (level 3). The expected salary must be a positive amount in VND per month. | `candidate`, `candidate_interest_job_category`, `job_category` — INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE | `CHECK` for a positive expected salary; trigger or stored procedure to validate the number and level of selected categories when the profile is completed. | Test 0, 1, 5, and 6 selected categories; select a category that is not level 3; test expected salary equal to zero or negative. |
| SC03 | The job-category hierarchy must have exactly three levels: a Job Group has no parent; a Profession has a Job Group as its parent; and a Specialisation has a Profession as its parent. The hierarchy must not contain cycles. | `job_category` — INSERT, UPDATE | `CHECK` for allowed category-level values; trigger or stored procedure to validate the parent level and prevent direct or indirect cycles. | Test an incorrect parent level; a Job Group with a parent; a category referencing itself; and direct or indirect cycles. |
| SC04 | An uploaded CV must have one of the following formats: `.doc`, `.docx`, or `.pdf`. Its file size must not exceed 5 MB. | `cv` — INSERT, UPDATE | `CHECK` constraints on `file_format` and `file_size_bytes`. | Test the three allowed formats and an unsupported format; test file sizes equal to 5 MB, greater than 5 MB, and zero. |
| SC05 | The CV used in an application must belong to the same candidate who submits that application. | `application`, `cv` — INSERT, UPDATE; referenced `cv` records — DELETE, UPDATE | Composite foreign key `(candidate_id, cv_no)` referencing `cv(candidate_id, cv_no)`. | Test an application using its candidate's own CV; test using another candidate's CV; attempt to delete or modify a referenced CV. |
| SC06 | An application may only be submitted for a job posting that is open, uses the on-site application method, and has not passed its application deadline. Contact name, email, phone number, and desired work location are required. The desired work location must be one of the locations offered by that job posting. The candidate must agree to the employer's personal-data usage agreement; AI-based CV analysis is optional. | `application`, `job_posting`, `work_location` — INSERT, UPDATE; `job_posting` and `work_location` — UPDATE, DELETE | `NOT NULL` and `CHECK` constraints for attributes within a table; trigger or stored procedure to validate job-posting status, application method, deadline, and work-location membership; database or application validation for consent. | Test open and closed job postings; on-site and external application methods; submissions before and after the deadline; valid and invalid work locations; consent enabled and disabled; missing contact information. |
| SC07 | A candidate may apply for the same job posting at most three times. Each new application must be submitted at least five minutes after the candidate's previous application for that job posting. A candidate cannot withdraw or delete an application after it has been submitted. | `application` — INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE | Trigger or stored procedure to validate the application count and the five-minute interval; authorization and procedure-level controls to restrict deletion. | Test the first, second, third, and fourth applications; test intervals shorter than, equal to, and longer than five minutes; test whether a candidate can delete a submitted application. |
| SC08 | Every employer must have exactly one verification task for each of the four task types. An employer is verified if and only if all four tasks are approved. Phone-number verification and personal-data agreement tasks are approved automatically and must not have an administrator reviewer. Company-information update and business-registration-document tasks require administrator review. When either manual-review task is approved or rejected, it must have a reviewing administrator and a review date. The review date must not be earlier than the submission date. | `employer`, `verification_task`, `administrator` — INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE | Composite primary key `(task_type, employer_id)` to prevent duplicate task types; trigger or stored procedure to ensure all four tasks exist and enforce task-type/status rules; `CHECK` for date ordering; calculate `isVerified` from all four task statuses if stored physically. | Test missing, complete, and duplicate task types; automatic approval with and without an administrator; manual approval/rejection with missing reviewer or review date; review date earlier than submission date; verify the `isVerified` result. |
| SC09 | If `expected_completion_date` is NULL, the recruitment need represents continuous recruitment. Otherwise, the expected completion date must be later than the declared date. The required number of employees must be at least one. The recruitment budget, if provided, must not be negative. | `recruitment_need` — INSERT, UPDATE | `CHECK` constraints for the required employee count, budget, and date ordering. | Test a NULL completion date; a date later than, equal to, or earlier than the declared date; employee counts of 0 and 1; NULL, zero, and negative budgets. |
| SC10 | A recruitment campaign may fulfil at most one recruitment need, and a recruitment need may be fulfilled by at most one campaign. A campaign may only fulfil a recruitment need declared by the same employer who created the campaign. | `recruitment_campaign`, `recruitment_need`, `employer` (and the declaration relationship, if retained) — INSERT, UPDATE | `UNIQUE` and foreign-key constraints for the optional one-to-one relationship; trigger or stored procedure to ensure that the campaign creator is the same employer who declared the recruitment need. | Test a campaign without a recruitment need; a campaign linked to a need declared by the same employer; a need declared by another employer; and two campaigns linked to the same need. |
| SC11 | A job posting must be posted for the company where the employer who created its recruitment campaign works. | `job_posting`, `recruitment_campaign`, `employer` — INSERT, UPDATE; related `recruitment_campaign` and `employer` references — UPDATE | Trigger or stored procedure to verify that the job posting's company matches the company of the employer who created its campaign. | Test matching and mismatching companies; change the campaign assigned to a job posting; change the campaign creator or the employer's company. |
| SC12 | A job posting may have no salary range when the salary is negotiable. Any provided minimum or maximum salary must be positive, and the minimum salary must not exceed the maximum salary when both are provided. The application deadline must be later than the posted date. A job posting whose application deadline has passed must have the `closed` status. Contact details are required when the application method is external. | `job_posting` — INSERT, UPDATE; scheduled status-maintenance task — SCHEDULED | `CHECK` constraints for salary, deadline, and conditional contact details; scheduled event/procedure or application logic to close expired postings. | Test NULL, zero, and negative salary values; minimum salary greater than maximum salary; deadline equal to or earlier than posted date; external application without contact details; expired posting that remains open. |
| SC13 | A job alert must have a non-empty search keyword and may only be saved after the candidate has confirmed consent to the use of personal data. Every job alert must have a consent-confirmation time. If a district is selected, a province/city must also be selected, and the district must belong to that province/city. | `job_alert` and related administrative-location data — INSERT, UPDATE; administrative-location reference data — UPDATE, DELETE | `CHECK` constraints for the keyword, consent, confirmation time, and province/city requirement; foreign key or trigger/stored procedure to validate district membership in the selected province/city. | Test an empty keyword; consent not confirmed or missing confirmation time; district with and without a province/city; district belonging to and not belonging to the selected province/city. |
| SC14 | An interview's end time must be later than its start time. An interview may only be scheduled for an application whose status is neither `accepted` nor `rejected`. Feedback may only be recorded when the interview status is `completed`. | `interview`, `application` — INSERT, UPDATE; `application` — UPDATE of status | `CHECK` for end-time ordering; trigger or stored procedure to validate the application status and the condition for recording feedback. | Test end time earlier than or equal to start time; applications with `submitted`, `viewed`, `interviewing`, `accepted`, and `rejected` statuses; feedback for `scheduled`, `completed`, and `cancelled` interviews. |
| SC15a | The employer conducting an interview must work for the company of the job posting associated with the application being interviewed. | `interview`, `application`, `job_posting`, `employer` — INSERT, UPDATE; related company/employer references — UPDATE | Trigger or stored procedure to verify that the conducting employer's company matches the company of the relevant job posting. | Test an interviewer from the same company and from a different company; change the application, job posting, or employer reference. |
| SC15b | An employer must not have two interviews with the `scheduled` status whose time intervals overlap. Two intervals that only touch at an endpoint are not considered overlapping. | `interview` — INSERT, UPDATE | `BEFORE INSERT` and `BEFORE UPDATE` triggers to check for another scheduled interview of the same employer using `new_start < existing_end AND new_end > existing_start`. | Test non-overlapping interviews; overlapping interviews for the same employer; overlapping interviews for different employers; overlap with a `completed` or `cancelled` interview; UPDATE that creates a scheduling conflict. |
| DA01 | `job_posting.application_count` must equal the total number of application records associated with the corresponding job posting. All applications are counted regardless of their application status. | `application`, `job_posting` — INSERT, DELETE, UPDATE of `job_posting_id` | `AFTER INSERT`, `AFTER DELETE`, and `AFTER UPDATE` triggers to maintain `application_count`; initialize the stored count from existing application records. | Test INSERT, DELETE, moving an application to another job posting, and updating an unrelated application attribute; compare `application_count` with `COUNT(*)` for each job posting. |

**Implementation principle:** Use primary keys, foreign keys, unique constraints, and check constraints whenever they are sufficient. Triggers should only enforce rules that cannot be adequately enforced by table-level constraints or the agreed stored procedures/application logic.
